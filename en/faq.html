FAQ

Here are the questions we are asked most often. If you cannot find what you need, please ask the branch directly.

How do I order?

How you order depends on the branch. The "Find a Branch" page shows which methods each branch accepts — LINE, telephone, or a delivery app. Please choose the branch nearest you.

Do you deliver?

Our Pattaya branch delivers with its own staff (within a limited area). Our other branches can be ordered from through delivery apps such as GrabFood, LINE MAN and ShopeeFood. The apps available differ by branch.

What is the cheapest way to order?

Ordering directly from the branch. Delivery apps add a fee, but if you order via LINE or by telephone you pay the prices listed on the menu.

Can I eat in?

Yes — you can eat in at any of our branches. Seating differs by branch, and some have counter seats only. For larger groups, we recommend checking with the branch in advance.

What payment methods do you accept?

In store we accept cash, credit cards and PromptPay (we do not accept LINE Pay). This may differ by branch, so if you need a particular method, please check with the branch beforehand. Orders through delivery apps follow each app's own payment methods.

Can I order in advance or choose a time?

Yes. You can specify a pickup or delivery time — just tell us the date and time you would like via LINE or by telephone. During busy periods we recommend contacting us early.

What sizes are your pizzas?

Two sizes: Regular (23cm across) and Large (30cm). Regular suits one person; Large is a good choice for two or three to share.

Is the menu the same at every branch?

The core menu is the same, but some items are not available at every branch. If there is something particular you want, please check with the branch first.

Can I buy frozen pizza?

Yes — we offer frozen pizzas to bake at home. 190 baht each (minimum five), or 900 baht for a set of five different flavours. Only some branches carry them, so please contact our Pattaya branch first.

Can I order in bulk for a party?

Yes. We will discuss the items, quantity and timing with you, so please contact the branch you plan to use directly.
