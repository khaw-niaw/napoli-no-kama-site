Why this pizza?

NAPOLI NO KAMA was born in Japan. We carry on its taste, its methods, and one phrase, here in Thailand.

With the heart of a strawberry —
we deliver taste and true delight.

A strawberry bruises at the slightest touch. We were taught that a customer’s heart is just as delicate. So with every pizza we honour a meeting that will never come again.

01

Over 24 hours for the dough

Thawed slowly in the fridge for 18 hours, fermented while we measure the temperature at its core, then rested another 6 hours. Dough made in a hurry never gives this aroma or spring.

02

Minutes in the oven

That dough, more than a day in the making, is baked fast in a high-temperature oven. The rim puffs and blisters; the centre stays moist. No home oven does this.

03

Before it cools

Cold pizza has no value — that is what we were taught in Japan. Our work is not done until it reaches you with the heat of the oven still in it.
